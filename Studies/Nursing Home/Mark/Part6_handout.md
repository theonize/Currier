# Mark 1:35-45 - Preaching in Galilee — Study Handout

## Verses

> **35** Early in the morning, while it was still dark, he rose up and went out, and departed into a deserted place, and prayed there.
>
> **36** Simon and those who were with him followed after him;
>
> **37** and they found him, and told him, “Everyone is looking for you.”
>
> **38** He said to them, “Let’s go elsewhere into the next towns, that I may preach there also, because I came out for this reason.”
>
> **39** He went into their synagogues throughout all Galilee, preaching and casting out demons.
>
> **40** A leper came to him, begging him, kneeling down to him, and saying to him, “If you want to, you can make me clean.”
>
> **41** Being moved with compassion, he stretched out his hand, and touched him, and said to him, “I want to. Be made clean.”
>
> **42** When he had said this, immediately the leprosy departed from him, and he was made clean.
>
> **43** He strictly warned him, and immediately sent him out,
>
> **44** and said to him, “See you say nothing to anybody, but go show yourself to the priest, and offer for your cleansing the things which Moses commanded, for a testimony to them.”
>
> **45** But he went out, and began to proclaim it much, and to spread about the matter, so that Jesus could no more openly enter into a city, but was outside in desert places: and they came to him from everywhere.
>
> — *World English Bible (WEB)*

---

## Overview

All of Capernaum had crowded Simon's door. Before dawn Jesus slips away to pray. Simon's group tracks him down: "Everyone is looking for you." He does not go back. He came to preach, so he moves on.

Then a leper comes, a man the law kept outside, away from home and worship. He kneels and says, "If you want to, you can make me clean." Jesus touches him and answers, "I want to." The man is clean at once. Jesus sends him to the priest and orders silence, but the man spreads the news. The outsider can now come back into town, and Jesus ends up outside. Even there, people keep coming.

**Big idea:** Jesus takes his direction from prayer rather than popular demand, presses on to preach, and by his own will, touch, and word cleanses a leper, doing what Scripture reserves for God, honoring Moses' law, and ending up outside in the outcast's place.

---

## Key Verse

> **41** Being moved with compassion, he stretched out his hand, and touched him, and said to him, “I want to. Be made clean.”

---

## Main Points

1. **Prayer before the crowd** (vv.35-37) — Jesus prays alone before dawn until Simon's party finds him.
2. **The herald moves on** (vv.38-39) — Jesus leaves the crowd to preach and cast out demons.
3. **The willing touch** (vv.40-42) — A leper leaves it to Jesus' will, and his touch and word cleanse him.
4. **Silence broken, places exchanged** (vv.43-45) — Told to say nothing, the man proclaims it, and Jesus is left outside while people come from everywhere.

## Info

<div class="info-columns">

### 1. Prayer before the crowd (vv.35-37)

#### v.35
- **After success:** the night before, the whole city was at the door (1:33).
- **"prayed"** in Greek means "was praying," prayer that went on for some time.

#### v.36
- **"followed after"** (Greek: καταδιώκω, *katadiōkō*) is strong: "tracked down." They break in on his prayer.

#### v.37
- **"looking for"** sounds like good news, but Mark often uses it of those who misunderstand or oppose Jesus (11:18). Eagerness for healing is not yet faith.

### 2. The herald moves on (vv.38-39)

#### v.38
- **"Let's go":** popularity does not set his agenda.
- **preach** (Greek: κηρύσσω, *kēryssō*) means to announce publicly, like a herald.
- **"I came out"** may mean he left town, or point to his whole mission (Luke 4:43).

#### v.39
- **Word and deed:** preaching announces God's reign, and driving out demons shows it at work.

### 3. The willing touch (vv.40-42)

#### v.40
- **leper** covered a range of skin conditions. The law required torn clothes, the cry "Unclean!" and living alone (Leviticus 13:45-46). Leviticus treats this as a condition, not guilt.
- **"you can":** Israel's king said only God could cure this (2 Kings 5:7).
- **"If"** may be doubt or deference. Either way, he leaves the choice to Jesus.

#### v.41
- **"compassion":** a few manuscripts read "angry." Either way, Jesus feels deeply and acts.
- **"touched":** touching the unclean made one unclean (Leviticus 5:3). Here the leprosy gives way.
- **"I want to"** is the man's own word, without the "if." Moses prayed. Jesus speaks by his own will.

#### v.42
- **"immediately":** Naaman had a journey and seven washings (2 Kings 5:14).
- **Not a promise** that every illness will be removed (2 Corinthians 12:7-9). Cleansings are signs of the Kingdom, whose fullness is still ahead (Revelation 21:4).

### 4. Silence broken, places exchanged (vv.43-45)

#### v.43
- **"strictly warned"** (Greek: ἐμβριμάομαι, *embrimaomai*) began as a word for snorting. This was no gentle goodbye.

#### v.44
- **"say nothing"** was for that moment. The risen Lord now sends us to speak (Acts 1:8).
- **the priest** could declare him clean, not make him clean. That opened the way home and back to worship. Jesus honors Moses.

#### v.45
- **"proclaim"** is Jesus' own verb (v.38). Mark never says whether the man reached the priest.
- **Eager, yet disobedient.** His news kept Jesus from entering towns openly.
- **"outside"** is where the law put the leper (Leviticus 13:46). Many see a hint of the cross here (Hebrews 13:12-13).

</div>

## Discussion

1. What does Jesus do before dawn, and how does he answer "Everyone is looking for you"?
2. What is the leper sure of, and what does he leave up to Jesus?
3. Jesus could have healed this man with a word. Why do you think he touched him?
4. Do you believe Jesus can help you, yet wonder if he wants to? Who near you feels left out?
