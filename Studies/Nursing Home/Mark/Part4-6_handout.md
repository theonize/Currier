## Overview

Jesus’ first disciples see his authority in a synagogue, at a sickbed, and across Galilee. His words free a man from an unclean spirit. His hand raises Simon’s mother-in-law. At sunset, people bring their neighbors for help. Mark distinguishes illness from demonic affliction; neither permits us to blame the sufferer.

Before dawn Jesus withdraws to pray, then moves on to preach. Popular demand does not determine his mission. Yet he stops for an excluded man, touches him, and makes him clean. The man’s publicity afterward hinders Jesus’ movement.

These healings reveal Jesus’ power and mercy, not a promise that every illness ends now. The woman’s service follows healing; it does not earn care. We may bring needs to Jesus, receive help, and welcome others without proving our worth through activity.

**Big idea:** Jesus’ authority restores; his mission calls for trust and obedience.

## Main Points

1. **Authority that frees** (vv.21-28) — Jesus teaches with authority and frees a man from an unclean spirit.
2. **Mercy that comes near** (vv.29-34) — Jesus heals at a sickbed and the doorway, silencing demons.
3. **Mission shaped by prayer** (vv.35-45) — Jesus prays, preaches beyond Capernaum, and cleanses an excluded man.

---

## Info

### 1. Authority that frees (vv.21-28)

#### v.21
- Jesus teaches in Capernaum’s synagogue.

#### v.22
- His authority astonishes.

#### v.23
- An afflicted man interrupts.

#### v.24
- Recognition is not faith.

#### v.25
- Jesus commands; no ritual.

#### v.26
- The spirit leaves.

#### v.27
- His deed demonstrates authority.

#### v.28
- News spreads through Galilee.

### 2. Mercy that comes near (vv.29-34)

#### v.29
- Authority reaches the household.

#### v.30
- Others bring her need.

#### v.31
- Mercy comes before service; dignity does not depend on usefulness.

#### v.32
- Sunset likely ends Sabbath restrictions.

#### v.33
- A town crowds the doorway.

#### v.34
- Healing and deliverance remain distinct.

### 3. Mission shaped by prayer (vv.35-45)

#### v.35
- Prayer precedes the next decision; no particular hour or posture is required.

#### v.36
- Simon’s group searches urgently.

#### v.37
- Popularity is not understanding.

#### v.38
- Jesus goes elsewhere to preach.

#### v.39
- Word and deliverance belong together.

#### v.40
- Ancient “leprosy” covered several skin conditions, not proof of personal sin.

#### v.41
- Jesus willingly touches and cleanses. Our care respects consent and medical safeguards.

#### v.42
- His command takes effect; this is no formula for guaranteed cures.

#### v.43
- Mercy includes a firm warning.

#### v.44
- The priest must certify cleansing, opening the way to community and worship.

#### v.45
- Disobedient publicity hinders Jesus.

## Discussion

1. What do Jesus’ words and actions reveal about him across these three parts?
2. Why does Jesus move on, and how does the healed man’s publicity affect his mission?
3. How can we bring needs to Jesus and welcome someone who feels alone, while receiving help ourselves?
